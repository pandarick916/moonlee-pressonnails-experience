## Prep is the Whole Game — and Everyone Skips It

Every single time someone tells me their press-ons fell off in three days, I ask one question: did you use a dehydrator? Nine times out of ten — no.

Here's the actual routine. Push back cuticles. Buff lightly. Swipe with nail dehydrator. Wait 60 seconds — full 60, not a vibe-based estimate. Then apply. Your nail beds produce oil constantly. The dehydrator neutralizes that. Skip it and you're basically applying stickers to a greasy pan. Don't skip it.

High water exposure? Dishes, long showers, swimming? Add a thin layer of nail glue along the edges every 4–5 days. That's it. That's the whole secret.

## FAQ

### What do Y2K nails look like?

Y2K nails are defined by holographic and iridescent finishes, almond or stiletto shapes, and color-shifting effects that look different in every light. Think: deep berry that turns purple in the sun, or icy blue that goes silver indoors. The aesthetic pulls from late-90s and early-2000s pop culture — think Destiny's Child, early Paris Hilton, that specific kind of shine that photographs like a music video. It's maximalist but wearable. That's the whole point.

### Are press-on nails worth it?

Yes — and the math is pretty simple. A salon set runs $60–$120 every 3–4 weeks. MOONLEE press-ons are a fraction of that, last just as long with proper prep, and you can do them at home in 20 minutes. The quality gap between press-ons and acrylics has basically closed. The price gap hasn't. Worth it doesn't even cover it.

### How long do Y2K press-on nails actually last?

With proper prep — 2 to 3 weeks. The variables: your natural nail oil production, water exposure, and whether you used a dehydrator. (You used a dehydrator, right?) MOONLEE's dual-bond adhesive is built for longevity, not just the first 48 hours.

### Are press-on nails safe for your natural nails?

Yes. The damage people blame on press-ons almost always comes from peeling them off dry. Don't do that. Soak in warm water for 10–15 minutes, or work cuticle oil around the edges first. MOONLEE's adhesive releases cleanly — no filing, no force, no drama.

### What nail shape is actually Y2K?

Almond. Not coffin — that's 2015. Not square — that's a whole different energy. Almond shape nail designs were everywhere in late-90s and early-2000s editorial, and it's the most functional long shape for real life. It's MOONLEE's signature shape for a reason.

### Can I type with long nails on?

Yes. Give yourself 2–3 days to recalibrate. You'll adjust. MOONLEE's almond tip tapers in a way that reduces keyboard snagging compared to square or coffin. I type thousands of words a week in these. You'll be fine.

Holographic finishes work across every skin tone — the color shifts with light instead of sitting flat. For deeper skin tones, the iridescent purple and blue shifts in Blackberry Macaron and Blue Sugar Macaron read especially vivid. For lighter skin tones, pink-to-white shift sets photograph the most ethereal. When in doubt — go holographic. It's literally designed to look good in every light condition.
