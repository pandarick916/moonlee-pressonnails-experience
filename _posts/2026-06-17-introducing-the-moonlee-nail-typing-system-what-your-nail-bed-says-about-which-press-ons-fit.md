## The MOONLEE Nail Typing System

Three dimensions. Every combination tells you something different about what will and won't stick to your nails.

**Dimension 1: Width**
Measure across the widest part of your nail bed.

- **Narrow** (<10mm) — Most standard press-ons run wide on you. You need brands that offer XS sizing, or you'll spend half your time filing down the sides.
- **Average** (10–13mm) — You'll fit most standard sizes. Still measure — "one size fits most" is doing a lot of work there.
- **Wide** (>13mm) — Standard press-ons leave a gap on the sides, which is where moisture gets in. You need [brands that actually accommodate wider nail beds](https://moonleehome.com/blogs/experience-with-press-on-nails/stop-the-c-curve-torture-pain-flat-nail-beds). [Blue Sorbet](https://moonleehome.com/products/blue-sorbet) and [Peach Panna Cotta](https://moonleehome.com/products/peach-panna-cotta) both have a flatter interior curve that sits better on wider nails without the edge gap.

**Dimension 2: Curvature**
Look at your nail from the fingertip — flat or arched?

- **Flat** — Nearly straight across. Press-ons with a strong C-curve will pinch and eventually pop off — this is the "lever effect." Your natural nail flexes, the rigid press-on doesn't, and the bond snaps at the weakest point.
- **Average** — Slight natural curve. Most press-ons work fine.
- **Curved** — Noticeable C-curve. Flat press-ons won't sit flush at the edges. You need nails with a pronounced interior curve, or you'll get lifting within days.

**Dimension 3: Oil Level**
How does your skin behave 2 hours after washing your hands?

- **Oily** — Your natural oils are actively breaking down the adhesive bond. 2–3x faster than normal skin. [Dehydrator before application isn't optional for you](https://moonleehome.com/blogs/experience-with-press-on-nails/stop-the-c-curve-torture-pain-flat-nail-beds) — it's the difference between 2 days and 2 weeks.
- **Normal** — Standard prep (alcohol wipe + buff) is enough.
- **Dry** — Longer wear, but more prone to brittle nails between sets. Use cuticle oil regularly.

## How to Find Your Nail Type

1. **Measure your nail width** — use our [free online sizing tool](/pages/press-on-nail-size-chart). 30 seconds, credit card works fine.
2. **Check your curvature** — bare nail, look from the fingertip. Does it arch or lie flat? Take a photo if you're unsure — it's easier to see in a picture than in person.
3. **Test your oil level** — 2 hours after washing hands, press blotting paper to your nail bed. Oil = oily category. No transfer = normal or dry.
4. **Match to products** — use the chart above to find your combination and the adhesive strategy that fits.

## What This Means for the Press-On Industry

Most brands are still designing for an average nail that doesn't actually exist. Wide nail bed users spend hours searching. Flat nail bed users deal with pain they've been told is normal. Oily nail bed users blame themselves when their $30 set falls off in two days.

A 2023 clinical review in the **Journal of Hand Surgery** found that nail plate morphology — curvature, width, thickness — varies significantly across individuals, and that poorly fitted artificial nails are a leading cause of onycholysis and bacterial entrapment. [1](https://www.jhandsurg.org/article/S0363-5023(23)00183-1/fulltext) Fitting one press-on shape to every nail type isn't just inconvenient — it's a documented health risk.

The AAD is equally clear: maintaining nail health starts with understanding your nail structure. Forcing an ill-fitting artificial nail onto an incompatible nail bed causes long-term damage. [2](https://www.aad.org/public/everyday-care/nail-care-secrets)

## FAQ: Nail Typing System

**Q: I have oily nail beds but also wide nail beds. Which problem do I fix first?**
A: Width first, always. An ill-fitting nail lifts regardless of how clean your prep is. Get the fit right, then deal with the oil.

**Q: Can I change my nail type?**
A: Width and curvature are structural — they don't change. Oil level does shift — hormones, seasons, skincare routine all affect it. If your press-ons suddenly stopped lasting as long, check whether your skin got oilier before blaming the brand.

**Q: What's the best press-on nail shape for flat nail beds?**
A: [Short oval or short almond](https://moonleehome.com/blogs/experience-with-press-on-nails/stop-the-c-curve-torture-pain-flat-nail-beds) — the tapered tip reduces leverage on the bond. Wide square shapes give the edge more surface area to catch and lift.

**Q: Is this a standardized industry system?**
A: Not yet — that's the whole point. Nobody has done this before for press-on nails. The more people use it and share their results, the more precise the recommendations get.

**Q: How do I measure curvature accurately?**
A: Hold your finger at eye level, look at the nail from the tip. Curves downward like a slide = curved. Extends straight out = flat. A photo works better than trying to feel it — most people can't accurately sense their own curvature.
