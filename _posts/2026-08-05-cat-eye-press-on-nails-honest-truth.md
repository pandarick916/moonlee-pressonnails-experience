Cat eye press-ons were everywhere on my For You page this spring — that glassy depth where the color seems to move when you tilt your hand. I wanted it. Bad. So I ordered two sets labeled "cat eye" and checked the tracking three times a day.

## What I Was Doing Wrong

When they arrived, both sets looked flat. No depth, no shift — just a printed stripe of shimmer sitting on top of a solid color. I held them under my desk lamp and tilted them every which way. Nothing moved. The "magnetic effect" was a picture of an effect, not the effect itself. I paid for the finish and got a photo of it instead.

## Why It Kept Happening

The thing that took me too long to figure out: real cat eye needs liquid. A nail tech applies gel while it's still wet, then drags a magnet through it to pull the iron pigment into that curved line. A press-on is already cured and solid — there's no liquid left to move. The only way to fake the shift on a finished nail is to embed or print a shimmer, and most "cat eye" press-ons just print a flat stripe. The AAD notes that the health of your natural nail matters far more than whatever finish sits on top[1](https://www.aad.org/public/everyday-care/nail-care-secrets), so I was buying the word, not the finish.

## How I Fixed It

Here's what actually worked:

**1. I stopped reading the words "cat eye" and started reading the finish.** The name on the box means nothing — what matters is whether the finish actually moves in light. Two finishes deliver that: **aurora** (a soft embedded shimmer that catches light at an angle) and **chrome** (a metallic, mirror-like reflect).

## FAQ

**Do you make cat eye press-on nails?**
Not yet — cat eye isn't in our lineup right now. What we make instead — aurora and chrome finishes — gives you that same light-catching depth without a salon visit.

**What's the difference between cat eye, aurora, and chrome?**
Cat eye is a magnet-pulled line in wet gel (salon-only on press-ons). Aurora is a soft embedded shimmer. Chrome is a full metallic reflect. Aurora and chrome are both available as finished press-ons; cat eye basically isn't, outside a printed stripe.

**How do I get the magnetic shift look without a salon?**
Skip the "cat eye" label and look for aurora or chrome finishes. They catch light at an angle and give you that depth from any finished press-on set.

**Do you make press-ons for wide or small nail beds?**
Yes — sizing per finger is how we do it. Our XS pinky is 6.08mm and our XS thumb is 10.68mm, not a vague "small." Wide and flat beds get the same per-finger treatment, so the finish sits flush instead of floating.

**Are aurora and chrome press-ons safe for my nails?**
Yes — and fit is most of it. The AAD notes that taking care of your natural nail under any artificial one is what prevents damage[2](https://www.aad.org/public/everyday-care/nail-care-secrets/basics/pedicures/reduce-artificial-nail-damage), and a correct, gap-free fit is most of that battle.
