A translucent tip lets more of what sits underneath remain visible. Depending on the tip, adhesive and application, you may notice tab edges, uneven coverage or a visible patch that was not obvious in the product photo. That does not automatically mean the set is defective, but it does not prove the application is correct either.

Before a brunch outing, turn your hand toward the window as you pick up your cup. If you like **jelly press on nails**, check whether the finish still appeals to you in that everyday light rather than judging it only under studio lighting.

**Start with the look you want.** More coverage can make the underside less visible, but it also changes the transparent effect. Do not paint the bonding surface or change the adhesive routine unless the manufacturers permit it.

## Choose Your Route

Which matters more to you: keeping the see-through finish or reducing how much of the underside you can see?

ROUTE A · KEEP THE TRANSLUCENCY

### Check the application, not a concealment hack

Review the permitted adhesive, fit and application instructions. Ask for a worn-on-hand photo or video if the listing only shows loose tips.

**The trade-off:** Some underside detail may remain visible through clear areas. Do not assume every adhesive can become invisible.

ROUTE B · PRIORITIZE COVERAGE

### Compare a more opaque finish

Consider a solid-color base or a design with more painted coverage. Judge the actual set in ordinary light rather than assuming a color name guarantees opacity.

**The trade-off:** More coverage changes the sheer look. Painted areas may obscure detail, while clear areas remain see-through.

## Before You Change the Application

Check the fit firstTry each tip without adhesive. It should sit comfortably without force or pressure on skin. Recheck the product's sizing guidance if the fit is wrong.Use the specified adhesive methodDo not choose a thinner layer simply to hide a mark. Follow the adhesive's quantity, placement, pressure and setting instructions.Leave the bonding surface as directedAdding polish to the underside introduces another surface between the tip and adhesive. Do not treat that as a universal fix; check compatibility with both manufacturers first.Deal with lifting rather than hiding itIf a tip is lifting or uncomfortable, follow removal guidance instead of masking the area with artwork, top coat or more adhesive.

## What a Photo Cannot Tell You

Lighting, opacity and the underlying adhesive affect what you see. A bright reflection or a smooth studio image does not establish how a set will look on your hand. The cover here is an editorial illustration, not a product photo or a demonstrated before-and-after result.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why can I see adhesive under a translucent tip?

A see-through finish can reveal details beneath it, including tab edges or uneven adhesive coverage. Check fit and the exact adhesive instructions rather than assuming one universal cause.

### Should I paint nude polish on the underside?

Not as a default fix. It changes the bonding surface. Only consider modifications permitted by the tip and adhesive manufacturers.

### Are opaque tips always better?

No. A more opaque finish can reduce visibility beneath the tip, but it changes the sheer look. Choose by your preferred finish and check the actual listing.

### Will artwork hide all adhesive marks?

No guarantee. Painted areas can obscure detail, but clear areas remain see-through. Artwork is not a solution for an incorrectly fitted or lifting tip.
