> ✨ **Vibe Check:** Ripping off your press-ons is literally ripping off your soul (and your keratin).
> 🧪 **No tearing. No prying. Especially NO dental floss.** The only secret: warm water, oil, patience.

## 🔎 Why Do Your Nails Turn White or Peel After Removal?

**You're rushing.**
The adhesive isn't fully broken down yet. You force it off anyway. Now you're peeling away the top layer of your nail plate. Those white, flaky spots? Not mold — that's damaged keratin. [1](https://www.allure.com/story/how-to-remove-press-on-nails)

## 💅 The MOONLEE 3-Step Ritual (Reusable & Safe)

**❶ Create a Tiny Gap**
Use a wooden cuticle stick to nudge a small edge on the side of the nail. Don't force it — just enough for the soak to get in. Too much pressure here bruises your nail bed.

**❷ The Warm Oil Soak**
Warm water + a squirt of dish soap + a few drops of cuticle oil or coconut oil. Soak 10–15 minutes. The soap breaks the bond. The oil does the rest. [2](https://lilacst.com/blogs/press-on-nails/how-to-remove-press-on-nails)

**❸ The Gentle Slide**
Nudge again with the stick. It should slide right off. If there's resistance — stop. Soak another 5 minutes. Never yank.

## 🆘 The 'Dental Floss' Hack is a Lie ❌

Reddit loves it. Your nails hate it. Sawing floss through the glue puts massive tension on your nail bed — you'll end up with paper-thin nails that split for weeks. Just don't. [3](https://www.healthline.com/health/how-to-remove-press-on-nails)

## ❓ FAQ

### How do I clean them to reuse?

Scrape off residue with your cuticle stick. Stubborn bits? A Q-tip with a tiny amount of **non-acetone** remover. Go light.

### Can I use acetone?

Only if you're tossing them. Acetone melts the acrylic. For reusable sets, oil-soak only.

### Do I need cuticle oil after?

Yes, always. Your nails just went through a lot — give them some love. Rub in cuticle oil right after and let it soak in.
