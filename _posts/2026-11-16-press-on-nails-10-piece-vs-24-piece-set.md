Nine tips fit. The last one rocks when you press it down, and there is no other size left to try. That is when the extra pieces in a larger box start to sound useful.

But extra tips help only if they fit. Here is how the ten-piece and twenty-four-piece sets in this comparison differ, and what to check before choosing either one.

The short version

- Ten pieces cover one full application, with no spares. A twenty-four-piece box offers more sizes to try.
- One Size at checkout does not guarantee that every finger will find a match.
- Compare the price with the tips you can use, including any that fit as replacements.
- Piece count does not establish quality or manufacturing method. This comparison covers the two products below.

## What the Number on the Box Tells You

A ten-piece set contains enough tips for both hands, with no extras. With Candy Cane Lane, you choose XS, S, M or L before ordering. Snowfall Ribbon contains twenty-four tips in a range of sizes, with no size label to choose at checkout.

The count tells you how many tips you get. It does not tell you how well they will fit or whether you will prefer the design. A larger box is not automatically a better purchase.

## The Ten-Piece Set Has No Spare. That Is the Trade.

If all ten tips fit, you use the whole set for one application. If one comes off intact, check the product's cleaning and reapplication instructions before wearing it again. If it cracks or goes missing, there is no spare in that box. Ask the seller about replacement options rather than assuming single tips are available.

After a full ten-nail application from a twenty-four-piece box, fourteen tips remain. Some may fit as replacements; others may be too wide or too narrow for the finger that needs one.

The AAD recommends not using your nails as tools.[2](https://www.aad.org/public/everyday-care/nail-care-secrets/basics/healthy-nail-tips) Use a tool, rather than a nail tip, to lift a stubborn tab or scrape off a label.

## What "All Sizes Included" Actually Removes

You do not have to choose XS, S, M or L to order this twenty-four-piece set. You still need to find a comfortable fit when it arrives.

If the smallest tip is too wide, check whether the maker allows filing. If the widest is too narrow, the extra pieces will not solve that. Where a full size range is available, compare it with your measurements before buying.

Think of the larger box as more sizes to try, not a promise that every hand is covered.

## Why These Two Sets Are Packaged Differently

Candy Cane Lane is a ten-piece handmade design offered in four size labels. Snowfall Ribbon is a twenty-four-piece set offered as One Size. That is the comparison here, not a rule that all handmade sets contain ten tips or that all larger sets are printed.

With the ten-piece format, you compare your measurements with a size group before ordering. With the twenty-four-piece format, you select individual tips from the box.

Neither count establishes the production cost or manufacturing method. Choose the artwork you like, then check whether the available sizes suit your nails.

## Compare More Than the Price Per Piece

Dividing the price by the number of tips tells you the cost of each piece in the box. It does not tell you how many fit your hands.

For another comparison, divide the set price by the number of tips you can actually use. If exactly ten tips in a twenty-four-piece box fit, use ten as the denominator. If more fit and can serve as replacements, include those too. The usable count depends on your hands, not just the packaging.

Use the current prices on the product pages below. Neither calculation tells you the cost per wear; that also depends on reuse and replacements.

I would compare fit and design first, then price. A cheaper box is not much use if you cannot find a comfortable tip for every finger. Equally, extra artwork is not worth paying for if it is not what you want to wear.

Two ways to compare the price

**Price ÷ tips in the box** gives you the price per supplied tip. It counts every piece, whether or not it fits.

**Price ÷ tips you can use** accounts for fit, including suitable spares. Keep the same definition when comparing the two formats.

| Calculation | 10-piece box | 24-piece box  ---  ---  Price per supplied tip | Set price ÷ 10 | Set price ÷ 24  Price per usable tip | Set price ÷ the tips that fit; use 10 only if all fit | Set price ÷ all tips that fit, including usable spares  What neither measures | Reuse, wear time or replacement availability | Reuse, wear time or whether spares fit a particular finger |

## What a Size Label Does Not Mean

An M set does not contain ten tips of one width. It groups different widths for different fingers under one label.

Compare millimeter measurements rather than carrying a size letter from one brand to another. If your thumb matches one label and your pinky another, check the available sizing options before ordering.

## How to Choose

If your measurements match a ten-piece size group and you like the artwork, that format gives you a set without extra tips to sort.

If you have not measured, start there. A soft tape, or a strip of paper and a ruler, lets you compare each finger with the chart. [See the measuring method and the XS–L size chart](https://www.moonleehome.com/pages/press-on-nail-size-chart?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=content&utm_campaign=press-on-nails-10-piece-vs-24-piece-set).

If you prefer choosing tips individually at home, consider the twenty-four-piece box. Check its available size information first, and remember that the remaining pieces may not fit the same fingers.

👉 Try each tip on before adding adhesive. Check width and curve without forcing the sides down. Surface fit matters to adhesive bonding,[1](https://www.sciencedirect.com/topics/engineering/cyanoacrylate-adhesive) but piece count alone does not establish a good fit or predict wear time.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is a 24-piece set better value?

It depends on the price and how many tips fit you. Compare the current set prices and the tips you can actually use, rather than dividing only by the number in the box. Extra pieces are useful as spares only when they fit the finger that needs one.

### Do I have to measure for a 24-piece set?

You do not choose a size label to order Snowfall Ribbon. Measuring can still help you check the available size range before buying. Once it arrives, try the tips on individually; "all sizes included" does not guarantee a match for every hand.

### Can I mix sizes inside one 10-piece set?

Candy Cane Lane is listed in XS, S, M and L. If your fingers do not fit one size group, ask about other sizing options before ordering. A mixed-size set or single-tip replacement should not be assumed.

### What if a tip comes off or breaks?

If it comes off intact, follow the product's cleaning and reapplication instructions before reusing it. Do not reapply a cracked tip. A ten-piece box has no spare; with twenty-four pieces, check whether a remaining tip fits before using it as a replacement.
