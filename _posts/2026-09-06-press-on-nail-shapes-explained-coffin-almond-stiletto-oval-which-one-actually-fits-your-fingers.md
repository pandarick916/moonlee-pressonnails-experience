## 🔍 Myth vs. Reality

**Myth:** Just pick whatever shape is trending on TikTok. If [coffin nails](https://www.moonleehome.com/pages/nail-glossary/coffin-nails) are everywhere, coffin nails are for you.

If you have ever stared at a shape chart and asked **what nail shape is best for me**, start with your job, not the trend. Type all day? If you type for a living, squoval and round are the only shapes worth considering — coffin and stiletto catch on keycaps mid-sentence and get pried off over thousands of keystrokes.

**Quick answer —** Don't follow the trend, follow your hands. Square suits long, narrow nail beds and makes short wide fingers look shorter. Almond narrows wide fingers with its tapered sides. If you type all day, squoval or round wins because there are no corners to catch on keycaps.

**❶ Wide Fingers / Chubby Hands**
Go **almond**. The tapered silhouette narrows the visual width of your fingers. Even a short almond works — you only need 3-5mm of free edge for the taper to do its job. **Oval** is your runner-up. **Avoid square** — flat, blunt tips emphasize width.[1](https://nailsami.com/blogs/guides/nail-shapes-guide-which-shape-suits-your-fingers-best)

**❷ Narrow / Slender Fingers**
Go **square** or **squoval**. The flat, wide tip adds visual bulk where your fingers need it. This is the one body type where squared-off shapes actually work in your favor.[2](https://nailatlas.com/guides/nail-shapes-guide)

**❸ Active Lifestyle (typing, gym, kids)**
Go **squoval** or **round**. Round has the highest durability rating — no corners to catch, no tapered weak points. Squoval gives you structure without the snag risk. **Stiletto is absolutely off the table** — those needle tips will snap the first time you open a can of soup.[3](https://www.cutiecure.app/nail-shapes-guide)

**❹ Office / Professional / Minimalist**
Go **oval**. It looks polished without screaming "I spent two hours at a salon." It's the default for the clean-girl aesthetic and the most universally accepted shape in conservative workplaces.[4](https://365nails.com/zh-hant/blogs/news/choosing-press-on-shape-for-your-lifestyle-2026-05-26)

## 😱 The "Common Sense" Trap

**"Longer nails look more elegant, so I should always go long."** This is how people end up with coffin nails that make their hands look like claws. Proportion matters more than length. On wide fingers, even 2-3mm of extra length creates a dramatic slimming effect — you don't need 20mm coffin talons. The dirty secret of nail salons: experienced nail techs will talk you out of the shape you asked for if it doesn't fit your hands. They just never tell you why.

**Q: Which press-on nail shape lasts the longest?**
A: Round and oval. No corners means no stress concentration points. Stiletto is the most fragile — the extreme taper thins the sidewalls to the breaking point. Square falls in the middle (sharp 90-degree corners catch and chip).[1](https://nailsami.com/blogs/guides/nail-shapes-guide-which-shape-suits-your-fingers-best)

**Q: Are coffin and ballerina nails the same shape?**
A: Yes, identical shape with different names. "Coffin" refers to the casket-like profile; "ballerina" refers to the pointe shoe silhouette. Same nail, different branding.[2](https://nailatlas.com/guides/nail-shapes-guide)

**Q: Can I change the shape of my press-on nails?**
A: Yes. Most press-ons made from ABS plastic or PMMA acrylic can be filed down. You can soften square into squoval with a glass file in about 60 seconds per nail. But you can't easily go from round to coffin — you'd need to add material, not remove it.
