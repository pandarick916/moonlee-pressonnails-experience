You either want that juicy, semi-translucent finish, or you're asking about the sticky tabs that hold press-ons on. ## Branch A: Jelly-Finish Press-Ons (The Look)

A jelly-finish set is a semi-translucent nail with that gummy, light-catching depth — think a peeled grape skin, not a solid coat of color. The catch nobody mentions: because the nail is see-through, it shows everything underneath. Your real nail color shows through. And if you used liquid glue, the glue line shows through too.

![Close-up of a vivid coral-red semi-translucent jelly-finish press-on nail showing gummy light-catching depth | MOONLEE](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0651/2210/7463/files/jelly-finish-vivid-color.webp?v=1785847808)

That is the see-through glue problem. Liquid adhesive leaves dots and streaks you can spot right through a jelly nail. The fix isn't a better glue; it's switching to our Jelly Tabs, a single even layer with no point buildup.

![Comparison of liquid glue dots showing through a translucent press-on nail vs clean Jelly Tab application | MOONLEE](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0651/2210/7463/files/liquid-glue-vs-jelly-tab-comparison.webp?v=1785847808)

It's also why a jelly finish is the least forgiving of a bad fit. Any gap at the cuticle or sidewall reads as a visible seam through the translucency — a seam you'd never notice on an opaque nude at a wedding screams through a jelly nail. That's exactly why measuring in millimeters matters more on these than on a solid color[1](https://www.aad.org/public/everyday-care/nail-care-secrets/basics/pedicures/reduce-artificial-nail-damage). The [why press-ons look fake guide](https://moonleehome.com/blogs/experience-with-press-on-nails/why-your-press-on-nails-look-fake-the-cuticle-fit-sidewall-coverage-and-shape-guide-to-making-them-look-real) breaks down what "real" means at the cuticle.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What are jelly press-on nails?**
Two things: a semi-translucent, gummy-finish nail (the look), and Jelly Tabs, the adhesive strips that hold press-ons without liquid glue (the hold). MOONLEE makes both, so one search covers the finish and the application.

**Do jelly press-ons show the glue underneath?**
They show liquid glue, yes — dots and streaks read straight through a translucent nail. That is the see-through glue problem. Switch to Jelly Tabs and you get one even layer with no point buildup, so nothing shows.

**Are MOONLEE jelly finishes custom-fit for wide or small nail beds?**
They're made to our standard XS-to-L range, about 10.5–11.4mm at the thumb and 6.08mm at the pinky for XS, up to roughly 13.11–14.32mm at the thumb for L. No XXS exists, so if your pinky is under 6mm you'll file the XS down or go custom. Measure in mm before you buy.

**Are jelly press-ons good for beginners?**
Yes, if you start with the tabs, not liquid glue — tabs are more forgiving and easier to remove. Just don't skip the measurement; a translucent finish hides nothing, so a wrong size shows immediately at the cuticle.
