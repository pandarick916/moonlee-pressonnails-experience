## Why Fit Is Everything

The #1 reason press-ons fail isn't the glue. It's the fit.

Wrong size = gaps at the cuticle, lifting edges, nails popping off in a day. I see it in my DMs constantly. And almost every time, the person guessed their size because they didn't have a ruler.

So I built a free tool that fixes that. No app. No printing. No ruler. Just your phone and a credit card.

## How to Measure Nail Size for Press-On Nails (30 Seconds)

Every credit card is the same size — 85.6mm × 54mm. The tool uses that to calibrate your screen, then you hold your finger up and get your exact nail width. This is the easiest way to measure your nail at home — no tape measure, no guessing.

1. Open the tool on your phone or desktop
2. Lay your credit card flat on the screen
3. Hold each finger up to the screen and match the edge
4. Write down all 10 fingers — your dominant hand is usually 0.5mm wider

## The Golden Rule of Sizing

**Between two sizes? Go up.** A slightly larger nail covers your nail bed fully — no exposed edges, no gaps at the sides. Even 0.5mm too small and the edges start lifting within days — that gap traps moisture, which is exactly how greenies start.

One thing most people get wrong: they measure the tip instead of the widest point of the nail bed. Measure the widest point. That's the number that matters.

## Sizing Tips by Nail Bed Type

**Wide or flat nail beds:** Size up one from your measurement, then use gel tabs instead of glue. Glue can't seal the side edges on flat beds — tabs flex where glue snaps off. If you've been struggling with **press-on nails for wide nail beds**, nine times out of ten it's the size, not the glue. → [Glue vs Tabs guide](https://moonleehome.com/blogs/experience-with-press-on-nails/press-on-nail-glue-vs-tabs-21-day-wear-myth)

**Narrow nail beds:** Stick to your exact measurement. Don't size down for a tapered look — gaps at the sides collect moisture and the nail lifts fast. You want clean edges, no skin overlap on either side.

**Small hands / pinky fingers:** Measure every single finger. The pinky almost always surprises people. **Press-on nails for small nails** aren't one-size-fits-all — even within the same hand.
