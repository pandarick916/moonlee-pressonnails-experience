> **TL;DR:** "21-day wear" is a marketing line. Liquid glue = rigid bond that cracks with moisture. 3M adhesive tabs = flexible, nail-safe, reusable. Tabs actually last longer when applied correctly.

Most people assume adhesive tabs are the "cheap disposable" option and liquid glue is the serious, long-lasting one. I thought the same thing — until I actually tested both.

Here's what happens with glue: the bond is rigid. The moment moisture sneaks in (a shower, sweaty hands, washing dishes), it cracks. And once it cracks? The whole nail pops. There's no fixing it.

The hidden part nobody talks about: most liquid glues on the market contain free **formaldehyde** and **cyanoacrylate** in concentrations that aren't medical-grade. [1] And to get glue to "stick permanently," you have to buff your natural nail first — every single time. Do that enough and you're stripping away your keratin layer for good.

## Why MOONLEE Kits Don't Include Liquid Glue

We use 3M adhesive tabs instead — the same adhesive technology used in medical and industrial applications. [2] They flex with your nail, survive moisture, and come off clean.

## 3 Steps for a Grip That Actually Lasts

Getting tabs to hold isn't magic — it's method:

1. **Heat activate** — rub the tab between your fingers for 10 seconds before applying
2. **Press slow and hard** — hold each nail for 30–60 seconds with firm pressure
3. **Stay dry for 30 minutes** — let the adhesive fully cure before any water contact

## Glue vs. Tabs — What Actually Happens

| | Liquid Glue | 3M Adhesive Tabs |

| Error Margin | Zero — once it's on, it's on | Repositionable within 10 seconds |
| Removal | Soaking required, risk of damage | Peels off cleanly in one piece |
