Last July I took a brand-new set to a pool day with my friends. An hour in, the sidewall on my middle finger floated up like a little raft. Water had slipped underneath, and the whole nail was just... hovering. I spent the rest of the afternoon pressing it down like I could will it back on.

## What Kept Happening

Every time I went near the water, at least one edge lifted. Not the whole nail — just the sidewall, peeled up enough to let a thin line of pool water creep in. By the end of the day that one finger looked like it had a tiny clear shelf sticking out.

## Why It Kept Happening

A lifted edge isn't a glue problem, it's a fit problem. The gap between the press-on and your nail is a doorway, and water follows the seam by capillary action — no gap, no entry. Most "one size" sets are off by 0.5mm or more on at least one finger, which leaves exactly that gap. The AAD points out that moisture trapped under artificial nails is what leads to trouble[1](https://www.aad.org/public/everyday-care/nail-care-secrets/basics/pedicures/reduce-artificial-nail-damage), and a loose fit is a doorway.

## How I Fixed It

Here's what actually worked:

**1. I measured every finger instead of guessing.** Our sets are sized per finger in millimeters — my XS pinky runs 6.08mm, my thumb 10.68mm — so every millimeter is accounted for. A nail that matches your width edge to edge sits flush — no gap. If you're flying with these, my [travel guide](https://moonleehome.com/blogs/experience-with-press-on-nails/press-on-nails-for-travel-vacation-how-to-pack-apply-make-them-last-your-entire-trip) covers the pack-and-apply routine.

## FAQ

**Are press-ons waterproof for the pool?**
Press-ons aren't waterproof — but a tight fit survives a full swim, a splash, and a cannonball. The goal is a fit tight enough that water has nowhere to go.

**What's the best press-on shape for swimming?**
Short round or short squoval. They end closest to your natural nail bed, with less overhang to catch water. Skip long stiletto and coffin for serious swim days.

**How do I keep water from getting under my press-ons?**
Measure every finger and wipe with alcohol before applying. No gap, no lift.

**Do you make press-ons for small or wide nail beds?**
Yes. We're a custom-fit handmade press-on brand for wide, small, and flat nail beds, and we size per finger — XS pinky 6.08mm up to wider thumbs — so the set sits flush whether your beds are small, wide, or flat. When the nail sits flat, water has no edge to catch.

**How long do they last at the pool?**
With a correct fit and bare-nail prep, I get 10–14 days, pool days included. I remove them when I'm ready, not because they failed.
