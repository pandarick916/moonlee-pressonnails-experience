Lay out the tips before opening the adhesive. Match them to your nails, keep the instructions nearby, and give yourself time to check the fit. Your first set does not need to be a race.

If you are learning **how to apply press on nails** before a weekend brunch, try each unglued tip while your hands rest flat on the table. That small pause makes it easier to notice an edge sitting on skin before you commit to adhesive.

**Quick answer:** Size, prep, then apply using the exact instructions for your set and adhesive. There is no universal glue countdown or guaranteed first-set wear time.

## Size, Prep, Apply: Your First-Set Checklist

THREE STEPS • NO GUESSWORK

1. **Size before adhesive.** Check each unglued tip against its matching nail. It should cover the nail without pressing onto skin or needing force to sit in place. Follow the brand's sizing instructions; filing cannot make a small tip wider.
2. **Prep for your specific product.** Read the set and adhesive instructions, gather the specified supplies and prepare a clean, dry workspace. Do not assume every glue or tab requires the same buffing or cleansing routine.
3. **Apply as directed.** Follow the adhesive's quantity, placement, pressure and setting instructions. Do not borrow a fixed waiting time from a different product. Read the removal method before you begin.

## First-Timer Mistakes: What to Do Instead

## Choose a Starter Set by Fit, Not Just Price

Look for usable sizes, clear instructions and a design you want to wear. Check which supplies are included and which you need separately. Neither a low nor a high price tells you whether the tips will fit your nails.

If a tip does not sit comfortably without force, check another size or shape before applying it. Follow the manufacturer's sizing guidance rather than automatically sizing down. A file removes material; it cannot make a narrow tip wider.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long will my first set last?

Wear time depends on fit, adhesive, application and daily use. Check the product's guidance instead of treating a particular number of days as a beginner guarantee.

### Do I need a UV or LED lamp?

Not for adhesive tabs or conventional air-drying nail glue. Some gel-based adhesives require a compatible lamp; check the exact product instructions before choosing an application method.

### Can I reposition a tip after applying adhesive?

Follow the adhesive's instructions. Do not force a bonded tip to slide or pull it off. If it is misplaced, use the specified removal method before trying again.

### What should I do if a nail lifts early?

Review the fit, application instructions, adhesive and daily use. Do not assume preparation is the only possible cause. Follow the product's removal guidance rather than forcing the tip off or covering the problem with more adhesive.
