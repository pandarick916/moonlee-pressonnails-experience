Handmade and factory-made describe production, not a guaranteed level of fit or wear. To choose between two sets, compare what you are actually buying: dimensions, finish, supplied sizes, replacement options, and the design you want.

This is a buying guide, not a 30-day wear test. It does not assign either category a fixed wear time or assume that a production label proves quality.

## Compare the Set, Not Just the Label

| Compare | For a handmade listing | For a factory-made listing  ---  ---  Sizing | Is it made to measurements, sold in fixed sizes, or supplied as a mixed-size set? | Which sizes are included, and do their widths and curves suit your nails?  Design | What is hand-finished, and are requested changes offered? | What pattern, finish, or raised details are actually supplied?  Contents | Check tip count, size selection, and application accessories. | Check tip count, usable spares, and application accessories.  Cost | Compare the current set price plus any customization and delivery costs. | Compare the current price and how many supplied tips you can use.  Wear and removal | Read the actual tip and adhesive instructions. Neither label establishes a universal wear time or damage-free removal.

## Handmade Does Not Automatically Mean Custom-Sized

A handmade design may be sold in standard size options rather than made to your measurements. Check the listing before assuming a maker uses your individual widths.

A mixed-size factory set can also fit well if the supplied tips suit your nails. In either case, width alone is not enough: dry-fit the base outline and curve without forcing the tip down.

## Detailed Art Is Not Exclusive to One Production Method

Hand painting or individually placed decorations can be a reason to choose a particular maker, especially when that maker offers the specific customization you want. But factory production is not limited to plain shapes. Compare the finished design rather than declaring one category incapable of raised details or complex patterns.

## What Are You Paying For?

### A particular design

If the color, composition, or detailing is the reason you want the set, compare it with the alternatives you would actually wear. Do not assume the extra cost purchases more days of hold.

### A practical setup

If you want an easy everyday rotation, compare suitable lengths, usable sizes, replacement options, and total cost. Factory-made is not automatically the lowest-cost choice for every buyer.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Are handmade press-ons more durable than factory-made ones?

The production label alone does not establish durability. Compare the actual tip construction, finish, care instructions, fit, and adhesive system. This guide does not report a measured wear-time winner.

### Are handmade press-ons always custom-sized?

No. A handmade set may use fixed size options. Check whether the seller offers individual measurements and which sizes are supplied before assuming it is made to fit your nails.

### Can factory-made nails have 3D designs?

Yes. Factory-made does not mean plain shapes only. Compare the actual raised details, pattern, and finish rather than using the production category as a design limit.

### When is a higher-priced set worth considering?

Consider it when the specific design, offered customization, suitable sizing, or contents matter to you. Compare the current total cost and usable tips, not a promise that a higher price guarantees longer hold.
